# GitHub Copilot Learning Path for HR & Operations

## Why This Path?

HR and Operations professionals own the processes that keep organizations running — onboarding, compliance, vendor management, reporting, and team communication. Copilot helps you generate consistent, professional documentation faster, automate repetitive formatting tasks, and build scalable process libraries your whole team can use.

This path focuses on documentation, templates, and workflow automation.

## Quick Wins This Week

1. **Job description** — Generate in under a minute:
   > "Write a job description for a [job title] at a [company size/type] company. Include: about us (2 sentences), about the role (3 sentences), responsibilities (6 bullets), requirements (5 bullets), what we offer (4 bullets)."

2. **Onboarding runbook** — Describe your process:
   > "Write a Markdown runbook for onboarding a new remote employee. Include: pre-start checklist, Day 1 agenda, Week 1 milestones, and a FAQ with 5 common new hire questions."

3. **Policy summary** — Simplify complex documents:
   > "Summarize this HR policy in plain language for a new employee who has never read a policy document: [paste policy]. Use bullet points and a reading level of grade 8."
