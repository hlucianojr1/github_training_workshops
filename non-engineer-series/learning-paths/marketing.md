# GitHub Copilot Learning Path for Marketing

## Why This Path?

Marketing professionals write constantly — emails, campaign briefs, landing page copy, social posts, press releases, and internal updates. GitHub Copilot dramatically accelerates this writing work, helps you maintain brand consistency at scale, and lets you generate multiple variants for A/B testing in seconds.

This path focuses on the writing, template, and documentation skills most relevant to marketing.

## Quick Wins This Week

1. **Product launch email** — Start with one sentence and iterate:
   > "Write a product launch email for [product name]. The new features are [list]. Audience: existing customers. Tone: excited but professional. Under 200 words."

2. **Social media variants** — Generate 5 at once:
   > "Write 5 Twitter/LinkedIn post variants announcing [product/feature]. Each under 280 characters. Vary the angle: one focused on the problem solved, one on a specific feature, one with a statistic, one with a question hook, one conversational."

3. **Campaign brief** — Describe your goal:
   > "Write a creative brief for a Q3 email campaign. Goal: reactivate churned users. Audience: customers who haven't logged in for 90 days. Include: objective, key message, CTA, success metrics, and timeline."
