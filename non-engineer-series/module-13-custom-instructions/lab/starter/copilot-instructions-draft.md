## Repository Purpose

This repository contains internal documentation, templates, and tooling for Meridian Health's non-clinical staff. It supports roles including patient coordinators, operations managers, HR business partners, and administrative staff.

## Organization Overview

Meridian Health is a regional healthcare network with 12 hospitals and 85 outpatient clinics. We serve 2.3 million patients across the Pacific Northwest. Our mission is accessible, compassionate care for all communities.

## Glossary

Key Meridian Health terms and abbreviations Copilot should know:

- **ADT** (Admission, Discharge, Transfer): The process of registering a patient when they arrive, documenting their movement within our facilities, and recording their departure.
- **Prior Authorization**: A requirement that a provider obtain approval from a patient's insurance company before providing a specific service, referral, or medication.
- **HRBP** (HR Business Partner): A senior HR professional aligned to a specific department or hospital who provides strategic HR support.
