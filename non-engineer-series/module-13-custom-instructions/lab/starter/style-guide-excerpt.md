# Meridian Health Writing Style Guide — Excerpt

**Version:** 3.2 | **Owner:** Communications & Brand Team

This excerpt covers the core principles for written communication across all Meridian Health patient-facing and staff-facing documents.

---

## Preferred Tone

- **Warm and professional** — We are a trusted healthcare partner, not a transaction processor
- **Accessible** — Our patients include elderly adults, non-native English speakers, and people in stressful situations
- **Empowering** — Language should help people understand what to do, not overwhelm them with caveats

---

## Reading Level Target

**Target:** Grade 8 (Flesch-Kincaid)

Use short sentences. Aim for an average sentence length of 15–18 words. Paragraphs should be 2–4 sentences maximum in patient-facing content.

---

## Forbidden Terms (with Plain-Language Alternatives)

| ❌ Clinical/Jargon Term | ✅ Preferred Plain Language |
|------------------------|----------------------------|
| Ambulatory | Walking / outpatient |
| Comorbidity | Other health conditions |
| Contraindicated | Not safe to use with |
| Hypertension | High blood pressure |
| Inpatient | Staying in the hospital |
| Myocardial infarction | Heart attack |
| Pharmacotherapy | Medication treatment |
| Precipitating factor | What caused it |
| Triage | Prioritizing care based on urgency |
| Utilization | Use |

---

## Formatting Rules

1. **Use "you"** — Address the reader directly. Say "Your appointment is…" not "The patient's appointment is…"
2. **Avoid passive voice** — Say "We reviewed your chart" not "Your chart was reviewed"
3. **Lead with the action** — Put the most important instruction in the first sentence
4. **Maximum sentence length:** 25 words
5. **Spell out every acronym on first use:** "Electronic Medical Record (EMR)"
6. **Use numbered lists for steps, bullets for options or features**
7. **Never use ALL CAPS** — it reads as shouting

---

## Before and After Examples

### Example 1: Test Results Communication

**❌ Clinical version:**
> "Laboratory results indicate elevated serum glucose concentrations consistent with hyperglycemic conditions requiring pharmacotherapeutic intervention."

**✅ Preferred version:**
> "Your blood sugar is higher than normal. Your doctor recommends starting medication to bring it down. We'll explain your options at your next appointment."

---

### Example 2: Appointment Instructions

**❌ Clinical version:**
> "Cessation of alimentation for a period of no less than 8 hours prior to the scheduled procedure is required for patient safety purposes."

**✅ Preferred version:**
> "Please do not eat or drink anything for 8 hours before your procedure. This keeps you safe during the process."

---

### Example 3: Insurance Notice

**❌ Clinical version:**
> "Prior authorization was not obtained for the aforementioned service, resulting in claim adjudication denial per the terms of the patient's insurance contract."

**✅ Preferred version:**
> "Your insurance company requires advance approval for this service. We did not receive approval before your visit, so the claim was denied. We are working to resolve this and will contact you within 5 business days."
