# Lab: Generate 3 Real-World Templates

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Module:** 03 — Templates for Every Occasion

## What You'll Build

Three complete professional templates — a product launch email, a job description, and a weekly status report — by completing the partially-written starters in `lab/starter/template-starters.md`.

## Setup

1. Open `lab/starter/template-starters.md`
2. Open GitHub Copilot Chat
3. Have a text editor ready to save your completed templates

---

## Task 1: Complete the Product Launch Email

**Context:** Luminary Analytics v2.0 is launching. The new features are: custom dashboards, real-time data refresh, CSV export, and shareable links. Target audience: existing customers who use the current version.

**Step 1:** In Copilot Chat, type:

> "Complete this product launch email template for Luminary Analytics v2.0. New features: custom dashboards, real-time data refresh, CSV export, shareable links. Fill in all [SECTION MISSING] blocks. Keep the tone professional but excited. Total email should be under 300 words."

**Paste the template** from template-starters.md as context (or use `#file:template-starters.md`).

**Step 2:** Review the output. Try one refinement:
> "Make the opening paragraph shorter — it should be 2 sentences maximum."

**Reflection:** Did Copilot capture the right excitement level? What would you change?

---

## Task 2: Complete the Job Description

**Context:** Meridian SaaS Co. is hiring a Senior PMM. The role owns GTM strategy for their core analytics product. Company is remote-first, Series B, 300 people.

**Prompt:**
> `#file:template-starters.md` Complete the Senior Product Marketing Manager job description in this file. The company is Meridian SaaS Co., a 300-person B2B SaaS company. Fill in the company description, missing responsibilities (competitive positioning, sales enablement content, customer insights, content creation), and missing requirements (writing skills, cross-functional collaboration, data-driven mindset, SaaS domain expertise). Also add a 4-bullet benefits section.

**Refinement prompt:**
> "Make the 'About Us' section feel more human and less corporate. It should sound like a place people actually want to work."

---

## Task 3: Complete the Status Report

**Context:** You're a PM at a software company. Your team completed user authentication work this week, is midway through a data export feature, and is blocked on a design review.

**Prompt:**
> "Complete this weekly engineering status report template. This week: completed user authentication refactor (owned by Sam Okafor), completed login page accessibility fixes (owned by Casey Kim). In progress: data export feature 60% complete (Drew Santos), mobile nav redesign 40% complete (Jordan Rivera). One blocker: design review for the export flow is delayed — waiting on Taylor Nguyen, expected resolution Friday."

**Fill in the metrics table** with realistic-looking numbers:
> "Fill in the metrics table for this report. The team completed 8 story points, closed 3 bugs, and average PR cycle time was 18 hours. The targets are 10 points, 5 bugs, and 24 hours."

---

## Bonus: Build Your Own Template

Pick a document you write repeatedly at work. In Copilot Chat:

> "Create a reusable template for [document type] that [your team/role] can use. Include placeholders in [SQUARE BRACKETS] for everything that changes each time. Add section headers for: [list the sections you need]."

Save the template to your own notes for future use.
